### Roles and Collaboration Strategy
To maximize our learning curve and ensure comprehensive coverage of the system, we adopted a dynamic role-distribution strategy. following **Agile/Scrum methodology**. 

Instead of rigid, isolated roles, every team member acts as an active engineer across the entire stack. However, for each sprint, specific members are designated as the **Domain Tech Leads** for assigned areas. The Domain Tech Lead is responsible for breaking down the workload, assigning tasks, and code review /pull requests for that specific domain. 

Our domain ownership is distributed as follows:
* **Laila:** Business Development, Project Management, UX/UI, and Frontend.
* **Abdullah:** System Design and DevOps.
* **Faisal:** Database Architecture and Quality Assurance.
* **Saad:** Backend and Cybersecurity.

### Communication and Tools
To maintain a structured and corporate-like workflow, we rely on the following tech stack for team operations:
* **Methodology:** Agile / Scrum (working in structured sprints).
* **Project Management & Tracking:** Jira and Miro.
* **Version Control:** GitHub.
* **Daily Communication & Meetings:** Discord.
